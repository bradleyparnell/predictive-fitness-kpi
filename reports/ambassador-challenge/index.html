What needs attention

The ambassador promo codes do not exist in the LevelField catalog

Every ambassador link carries TDAMBREF or RDAMBREF. Neither code appears in LevelField's promotion list, while every converting code does, including DANMARK2 with 85 redemptions and WORLDTRI with 45. If these links have no offer attached behind the email capture, visitors hit a dead end and zero checkouts is a plumbing failure rather than an ambassador failure. This should be verified with whoever built the landing page before any conclusion is drawn about ambassador performance.

Eighty three percent of the roster has never shared

Forty eight ambassadors hold a working link and have produced nothing. Most indicated on the sign up form that they intended to post on Instagram, Facebook, and Strava. The gap is activation and reminder, not recruitment. Three people account for 18 of the 24 leads, and Jack Lucas generated six of his eight on a single day.

Recommended next steps

  1. Verify the offer behind TDAMBREF and RDAMBREF. Confirm the landing page attaches a live promotion. Until that is settled, the zero checkout figure cannot be read as an ambassador result.
  2. Look up the 24 captured emails in LevelField user records. Promotions analytics stops at checkout, so it cannot show whether any of these leads later started a trial or subscribed through another path.
  3. Run an activation push to the 48 inactive ambassadors. Resend the link with ready made copy and images. A single reminder against a group this size is the cheapest available lift.
  4. Study what the top referrers do differently. Triatlon Danmark and Matt Davies convert at over 60% on the same mechanics. Whatever their placement and offer looks like is the template worth copying.
