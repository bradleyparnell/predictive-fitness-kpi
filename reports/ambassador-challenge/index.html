How this program is measured and why checkout data does not apply

Ambassador links are standard 14 day trial links

They do not carry a discounted checkout offer, so the landing page analytics behind this page record an email capture and stop there. No checkout event is expected, and the absence of one is not a performance signal.

The success measure for the Ambassador Challenge is a subscription activated after the free trial. That requires joining referral source to trial and subscription records, which is a separate pull from the analytics shown here.

Maturity, and what can be said today

Trials need 14 days to resolve, and ambassador activity runs into mid September. Only the earlier leads have had time to convert, so any activation rate calculated now would understate the program.

What is measurable today is reach and participation: links issued, ambassadors sharing, and leads captured. Those figures are complete and verified.

What needs attention

Most of the roster has never shared

ambassadors hold a working link and have produced nothing. Most indicated on the sign up form that they intended to post on Instagram, Facebook, and Strava. The gap is activation and reminder, not recruitment. The top three sharers account for of all leads, and Jack Lucas generated six of his eight on a single day.

Two lead counts, and why they differ

Activation reporting is not yet wired up

Referral source is tracked at the landing page, but connecting it through to trial starts and paid subscriptions requires a separate join against user records. Until that exists, the program can be reported on reach and leads but not on revenue outcome.

Recommended next steps

  1. Build the trial to activation view by referral source. Join referral code to trial starts and subsequent paid subscriptions so the leaderboard can rank on activations rather than email captures.
  2. Look up the captured leads in LevelField user records. Confirm how many started a trial, and how many of the matured trials converted.
  3. Run an activation push to the inactive ambassadors. Resend the link with ready made copy and images. A single reminder against a group this size is the cheapest available lift.
  4. Study what the highest volume sharers do differently. The top three produced most of the leads. Whatever their placement and messaging looks like is the template worth giving the rest of the roster.
